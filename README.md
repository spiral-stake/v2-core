# Spiral Stake - Borrow OR Maximize

Holders of staked stablecoins can now borrow stblUSD against their staked stablecoins or maximize their yield via our efficient looping mechanism.

stblUSD is a dollar-pegged, overcollateralized exogenous stablecoin that is minted and burned algorithmically.

Supported Collaterals to mint stblUSD 1. sfrxUSD 2. sUSDe 3. sUSDf 4. Upcoming Staked stablecoins & their Pendle PTs
