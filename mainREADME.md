# Spiral Stake - Stable Leveraged Yields

Spiral Stake is a non-custodial leverage looping protocol for correlated yield-bearing assets on Morpho. For eg: stETH/ETH, sUSDe/USDC, PT-sUSDe/USDC, and more.

Lock in fixed or variable yield from staked stablecoins, staked ETH, Pendle PTs, or any correlated yield-bearing asset and then flash-leverage at a safer LTV to amplify returns without compromising security.
